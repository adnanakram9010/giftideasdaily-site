Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Gift Ideas Daily ("we", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Browsing data: like most websites, our hosting provider may log standard technical information (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and analytics.
  • Information you provide: if you contact us or subscribe to our newsletter, we receive the details you share (such as your name and email address).

Cookies

We may use basic cookies to remember site preferences. Third-party partners (such as advertising networks, once enabled) may use cookies to serve relevant ads. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

Affiliate links

Links to retailers such as AliExpress may include an affiliate identifier. When you click them, the retailer may set cookies to attribute any resulting purchase to us, per their own privacy policies.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only as needed to operate the site (hosting, email delivery) or when required by law.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us via our contact page.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the "last updated" date above will reflect the latest version.
