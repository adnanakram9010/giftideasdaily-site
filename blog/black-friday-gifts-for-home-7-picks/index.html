Black Friday Gifts for the Home: 7 Picks to Buy Today

By Gift Ideas Daily Editors · Nov 27, 2026 · 6 min read

Here is the short answer: the best Black Friday gift for the home is something the whole household uses every day — a robot vacuum that cleans on its own, a video doorbell that answers the door, or a chunky throw blanket that makes the sofa the best seat in the house. The seven picks below are all real gifts from our collection, priced from $13.05 to $165.59 — $414.31 in total. Black Friday is today, 27 November, and the AliExpress Black Friday sale runs to 3 December, with Daraz Blessed Friday running to 3 December too — this is the cheapest week of the year to buy them. Order by around 18 December for Christmas-morning delivery. Browse all our Christmas gift ideas alongside this list.

1. Smart Robot Vacuum ($165.59)

This is the big one on the list, and the one that changes daily life the most. The Smart Robot Vacuum ($165.59) cleans the floors on its own — under the sofa, around the table legs, through the hallway — while the household gets on with everything else. It is the gift for the busy parent and the pet owner who sweeps up hair every day. On Black Friday, a big-ticket item like this makes sense: it is the most expensive pick here, and the one that saves the most time, every single day, for years. Buy it for a household, not just one person, and the whole family benefits. More gifts for the home.

2. Indoor Herb Garden Kit ($64.69)

For the person who loves cooking — or the one who keeps saying they want to start — this kit turns a kitchen windowsill into a small herb garden. The Indoor Herb Garden Kit ($64.69) grows fresh basil, mint, and parsley indoors all year round, so the cook in the house can snip what they need instead of buying a bunch that wilts in the fridge. It is a thoughtful gift for a new homeowner, a keen cook, or anyone who likes their home to feel green in the middle of winter. It also looks good on a countertop. More gifts for parents.

3. Smart Video Doorbell ($60.39)

A video doorbell is one of those gifts people never think to buy for themselves, and then wonder how they lived without. The Smart Video Doorbell ($60.39) lets the household see who is at the door from a phone, whether they are in the kitchen or out of the house entirely. It is a practical gift for parents, for grandparents who live alone, and for anyone who orders deliveries and misses the knock. A sensible Black Friday buy — genuinely useful home tech, not a novelty. More gifts for the home.

4. Smart LED Strip Lights ($45.91)

Some gifts change how a whole room feels, and this is one of them. The Smart LED Strip Lights ($45.91) stick behind a television, along a desk, or around a bed frame, and change the colour and mood of a room with a tap on a phone. They are a favourite with teenagers and students, but they suit any household that spends winter evenings at home — warm amber for a film night, soft white for reading. Small, easy to wrap and install, and useful from day one. More gifts for teens.

5. Hand-Knit Chunky Throw Blanket ($43.89)

Winter evenings belong to the sofa, and a good blanket is what makes that spot worth fighting over. The Hand-Knit Chunky Throw Blanket ($43.89) is thick, soft, and made to be draped over the back of a sofa or folded at the foot of a bed — the kind of blanket that makes a whole room feel warmer just by being in it. It is a wonderful gift for a couple settling into a new home, for parents who spend evenings reading together, and for anyone who is always cold. If you want one gift that says "make yourself at home," this is it. More gifts for couples.

6. Ultrasonic Aroma Diffuser ($20.79)

A home that smells good feels good, and this is the simplest way to give a household that feeling every day. The Ultrasonic Aroma Diffuser ($20.79) fills a room with a soft mist of a favourite scent — lavender for calm evenings, citrus for bright mornings — and it doubles as a gentle night light. It is the gift for the person who loves their home, the one who is always lighting a candle, and anyone who wants a calmer living room after a long day. Buy it with the throw blanket above and you have a complete cosy-evening gift set. More gift ideas for her.

7. Himalayan Salt Lamp ($13.05)

The smallest price on this list, and one of our most-loved home gifts. The Himalayan Salt Lamp ($13.05) gives off a warm, amber glow that makes a whole room feel calmer the moment it is switched on — perfect for bedside tables, reading corners, and winter evenings when the main lights feel too harsh. It suits almost any room, which makes it the ideal Black Friday filler: the extra gift that rounds out a bigger present, or a thoughtful small gift for a host. It keeps working long after Christmas is over, and it changes how a room feels rather than just filling a drawer. More Christmas gift ideas.

How to pick a Black Friday gift for the home

The rule is simple: pick something the whole household will use, not just one person. The robot vacuum and the video doorbell are for the household that wants life to run more smoothly. The herb garden and the LED strip lights are for the home that is still being made personal. And the throw blanket, the diffuser, and the salt lamp are for the home that deserves to feel warmer and calmer through winter. Frequently asked questions

What is the best Black Friday gift for the home? Something the whole household uses every day — a smart robot vacuum, a video doorbell, or a chunky throw blanket. The best home gifts save time or add comfort, rather than sitting on a shelf.

When do the Black Friday sales end? The AliExpress Black Friday sale runs to 3 December 2026, and Daraz Blessed Friday runs 26 November to 3 December. Discount codes are limited in quantity, so the checkout page always has the final price.

Will these gifts arrive before Christmas? Order by around 18 December for the best chance of Christmas delivery, and always check the delivery estimate at checkout before you buy.

Are these gifts only for homeowners? No — most of them suit renters too. The diffuser, the salt lamp, the throw blanket, and the LED strip lights need no installation and move easily, which makes them safe choices for anyone still setting up.

Buy them while Black Friday prices last, and give the household on your list something they will actually use every day.
