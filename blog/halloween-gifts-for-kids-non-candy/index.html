Halloween Gifts for Kids: 8 Non-Candy Picks That Beat the Trick-or-Treat Haul

By Gift Ideas Daily Editors · Oct 5, 2026 · 6 min read

Let's be honest: the trick-or-treat haul is a sprint, not a marathon. By November 2nd it's either gone or sitting in a bowl being picked over with diminishing enthusiasm. A Halloween gift, on the other hand — something wrapped, something chosen — turns the season into a memory. These eight non-candy picks are the ones kids actually keep touching after October ends. See all our Halloween picks here.

1. A shockproof kids' camera

At $49.99, a Shockproof Kids' Camera is the gift that turns every Halloween party into a documentary. Kids photograph their costumes, their friends, the decorations — and parents end up with a photo album that no phone-camera snapshot could replace. Built for drops, spills, and small hands.

2. A build-your-own robot kit

The Build-Your-Own Robot Kit ($44.95) is October's best rainy-afternoon project: a few hours of building followed by a robot that actually moves. For kids who like making things, this beats a bag of candy by a mile. More gift ideas for kids.

3. A galaxy star projector for their bedroom

The Galaxy Star Projector Light ($45.99) turns a dark October bedroom into a spooky-then-soothing light show — haunted nebula for the costume try-on, calming stars for bedtime. It works every night of the year, which is the whole point: gifts with a second life win.

4. A dinosaur terrarium night light

The Dinosaur Terrarium Night Light ($32.99) is part night light, part desk pet. Dark October nights are exactly when a small glowing dinosaur habitat feels magical — and it doubles as the perfect post-bedtime reassurance for kids who find the season a little too spooky.

5. Smart LED strip lights for their room

At $29.99, Smart LED Strip Lights are the teen-and-tween-approved pick: they can set the whole room to pumpkin orange for October, then switch to any color they want for the rest of the year. App-controlled, no ladder, instant room transformation. More gifts for teens.

6. Glow-in-the-dark Halloween pajamas

The Matching Family Halloween Pajamas ($19.62) with a glow-in-the-dark ghost print are the kind of gift that becomes a tradition — worn on Halloween night, photographed every year, and soft enough for actual sleep. Under twenty dollars and endlessly re-giftable to growing kids.

7. A ready-made "Boo" Halloween gift box

For the "I don't know what to get" crowd: the Spooky "Boo" Halloween Gift Box ($44.25) is a curated seasonal box that's already packed and presentation-ready. Zero wrapping, zero guesswork, maximum Halloween energy.

8. A Halloween window projector

The Halloween Window Wonderland Projector ($41.53) projects animated Halloween scenes onto any window — ghosts, pumpkins, witches — turning the whole house into the neighborhood's favorite stop. It's a gift for the kid and a spectacle for the street.

The non-candy rule

The best Halloween gifts for kids share one trait: they outlive the season. A camera, a robot, lights, pajamas — all of them are still getting used in January. That's what separates a gift from a treat. Take the Gift Finder Quiz to match any kid in 60 seconds.
