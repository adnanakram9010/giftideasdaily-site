Black Friday Gifts for Kids: 7 Picks They'll Love

Here is the short answer: the best Black Friday gift for a child is something they will actually play with, not something that looks good in a photo and then gathers dust. 1. Shockproof Kids' Camera ($41.39)

Children love taking photos, and they love it even more when the camera is actually theirs. The Shockproof Kids' Camera ($41.39) is built for small hands and hard floors: it survives drops, bumps, and being stuffed into a school bag, and it takes real photos children can be proud of. It is the gift for the child who photographs everything — the dog, their drawings, their friends in silly hats — and it gives parents a photo album no phone snapshot could replace. The Personalized Kids Backpack ($10.15) is a sturdy little bag printed with their name, so it is unmistakably theirs at school, at nursery, and on every family trip. It is the gift for the child starting school, the one who loses everything, and the one who loves having something that belongs to nobody else. Dark winter nights are exactly when a small glowing dinosaur habitat feels magical. The Dinosaur Terrarium Night Light ($9.62) is part night light, part desk pet: a tiny dinosaur world that glows softly beside the bed, giving just enough light for a child who is not quite ready for total darkness. It is the gift for the dinosaur-obsessed child, the one who is a little bit scared of the dark, and the one whose bedroom could use something more interesting than a plain lamp. Ask any parent what toy actually gets played with for more than a week, and magnetic tiles are usually near the top of the list. The Mini Magnetic Tile Toys ($9.15) are colourful tiles that click together into houses, towers, cars, and whatever else a child's imagination comes up with — and then come apart just as easily for the next idea. 7. Cute Fruit Silicone LED Night Light ($4.82)

Sometimes the best gift is the small one that makes a child smile every night. The Cute Fruit Silicone LED Night Light ($4.82) is a soft, squishy strawberry, banana, or peach that glows gently on a bedside table — cute enough for a stocking, soft enough to squeeze, and safe enough to leave on all night. How to pick a Black Friday gift for a child

The rule that ties this list together is simple: pick something they will use, not something that will impress the adults in the room. The camera and the drawing table are for the creative child. The karaoke machine is for the performer. The backpack is for the child who loves things that are theirs alone. The dinosaur light and the fruit light are for the child who needs a little comfort at bedtime. And the magnetic tiles are for every child who likes to build — which is most of them.
