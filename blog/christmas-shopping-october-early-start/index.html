Start Your Christmas Shopping in October: What to Buy First (and Why Early Shoppers Win)

By Gift Ideas Daily Editors · Sep 28, 2026 · 6 min read

Nobody talks about October as a Christmas shopping month, and that's exactly why it's the best one. The shelves are full, the personalized-gift workshops have empty queues, the shipping cutoff panic hasn't started, and you can actually think about what people would love instead of grabbing whatever's left on December 22nd. Early shoppers don't just win on stress — they win on gift quality.

Here's what to buy in October, in order of urgency. Keep our full Christmas gift guide open alongside this list.

First: anything personalized

This is non-negotiable. Custom gifts have to be made before they're shipped, and October is when craftspeople have time for your order. A personalized family tree print ($54.95) or a custom pet portrait ($59.00) ordered now arrives with weeks to spare — ordered in December, it's a coin flip. Our rule: if it gets engraved, printed, embroidered, or painted, buy it in October.

A personalized Christmas ornament for the tree

At just $19.95, a personalized Christmas ornament is the single best "early gift" on the market: it's cheap, it ships with zero rush, and it hangs on their tree every December for a decade. Buy one for every family member now and your December shopping list gets noticeably shorter.

A custom photo puzzle for grandparents

The custom 1000-piece photo puzzle ($34.95) is a sleeper hit with grandparents and puzzle people — but it needs production time for the photo print. October order, December assembly around the family table. Perfect.

A personalized storybook for the kids

Kids' personalized gifts sell out first and disappoint hardest when they don't arrive. A personalized adventure storybook ($34.99) with the child as the hero, ordered now, is Christmas-morning magic with zero risk. It also works beautifully as a birthday surprise year-round.

An engraved leather keychain for the stocking-stuffer stash

Stocking stuffers are where December shoppers bleed money — $14.95 engraved leather keychains bought in bulk in October look bespoke, cost nothing, and cross five people off your list in one order. Future you says thanks.

Then: the big-ticket items before prices spike

October is also when to grab the expensive tech before holiday demand moves prices. A smart digital photo frame ($129.99) is the classic example — it's one of our most-gifted items precisely because it keeps giving (auto-updating family photos), and buying early means you dodge the December rush pricing. Same logic for anything popular: the whiskey decanter set ($69.95) for the home bar, the chunky-knit throw blanket ($119.00) for cozy season.

The October advantage, in one line

Buy personalized gifts in October for the lead time, buy popular gifts in October for the price, and enjoy December like a person instead of a warehouse employee. Still not sure what anyone wants? Take the gift finder quiz and let us do the thinking.
