Last-Minute Halloween Gifts: 8 Quick Picks

By Gift Ideas Daily Editors · Oct 26, 2026 · 6 min read

Halloween is five days away, and if you still have a gift, a party contribution, or a trick-or-treat stash to sort out, you are not late — you are just shopping in the final stretch. The trick to last-minute Halloween shopping is to stop hunting for the perfect big gesture and pick small things that do a job straight away: something that lights up the porch, something that fills a treat bag, something that makes a kid's face light up on the night itself. Here are eight quick picks from our collection that fit exactly that brief, all small, simple, and ready to go. Browse the full Halloween gift guide alongside this list.

1. Spooky 'Boo' Halloween Gift Box ($6.29)

This is the easiest last-minute gift of the lot: a ready-made Halloween box that looks like you planned it weeks ago. Hand it to a neighbour, a colleague, or a friend hosting the party, and you have covered the whole occasion in one small box. No wrapping, no assembly, no second trip to the shops — just a tidy, spooky little gift that is ready the moment it arrives.

2. Halloween Window Wonderland Projector ($41.59)

If the house still looks undecorated, this is the fastest fix available. Point it at a window and the whole front of the house turns into a moving Halloween display in minutes — no ladders, no tangled lights, no carving pumpkins in the dark. It is the gift for the person who loves Halloween but ran out of decorating weekends, and it doubles as the party backdrop once everyone is inside.

3. Halloween LED Candles (Skeleton Ghost Hands) ($1.88)

Real candles and excited children are a combination nobody needs on Halloween night, which is why these flameless skeleton-hand candles are such a smart last-minute buy. Scatter them along a windowsill, inside a lantern, or down the middle of the party table, and you get the flickering glow without a single safety worry. At under two dollars, they are also the easiest way to make a whole room feel like Halloween.

4. Halloween Ghost Glowing Night Light ($13.25)

Some Halloween gifts are only for one night; this one earns its place all year round. A friendly glowing ghost that works as a night light is perfect for a child who is excited about Halloween but still wants something gentle beside the bed when the costumes come off. It is a small, thoughtful pick for nieces, nephews, and godchildren — spooky enough for the season, comforting enough for every night after.

5. Pumpkin Lantern Candle Lamp ($3.30)

Nothing says Halloween faster than a glowing pumpkin, and this little lantern delivers that look without any carving at all. Pop it on the porch, in a window, or beside the front door, and the house instantly looks like it joined in. It is also a great filler gift: cheap, cheerful, and exactly the kind of thing that makes a Halloween hamper feel finished.

6. Halloween Candy Bags (10pcs) ($1.92)

Every trick-or-treat plan falls apart without something to put the sweets in, and these ten themed bags solve that problem for the whole street in one purchase. Hand one to each child as they head out, or use them to portion out treats for party guests. They are the least glamorous item on this list and probably the most useful — the practical gift that saves a last-minute scramble on the night itself.

7. Ghost Facing Plush Toy ($17.15)

For the child who wants a Halloween keepsake rather than another bag of sweets, a soft ghost plush is a lovely choice. It is cuddly rather than scary, which makes it right for younger children, and it survives long after the decorations come down — a toy that gets kept, not thrown away with the party leftovers. If you are buying for a little one you do not see often, this is the pick that feels personal.

8. Halloween Glow Bouncing Balls ($3.39)

Glow-in-the-dark bouncing balls are the classic party-bag filler for a reason: children love them, they cost very little, and they keep working after the party ends. Drop a few into treat bags, scatter them as table surprises, or hand them out to trick-or-treaters for something a bit different from sweets. Small, fun, and instantly Halloween — exactly what a last-minute list should end on.

Quick picks, zero panic

The pattern across all eight picks is simple: small, ready, and useful on the night itself. Pick one showpiece like the window projector, add a few low-cost fillers like the candles, candy bags, and glow balls, and you have Halloween covered without a single stressful shopping trip.
