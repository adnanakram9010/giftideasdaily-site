Cyber Monday Gifts: 7 Tech Picks to Grab Before They End

By Gift Ideas Daily Editors · Nov 28, 2026 · 6 min read

Here is the short answer: the best Cyber Monday gifts are the tech gifts people actually use every day — a mini projector that turns any wall into a cinema, a smart photo frame that keeps family photos updating on its own, or a telescope that makes a clear winter night feel like an adventure. The seven picks below are all real gifts from our collection, priced from $9.25 to $113.49 — $413.47 in total. Cyber Monday is 30 November, and the AliExpress Black Friday sale runs to 3 December, with Daraz Blessed Friday running to 3 December too — this is the cheapest week of the year to buy them. Order by around 18 December for Christmas-morning delivery. Browse all our Christmas gift ideas alongside this list.

1. Portable Mini Projector ($110.39)

This is the showpiece of the list, and the gift that gets used long after Christmas. The Portable Mini Projector ($110.39) turns any blank wall — or a white sheet in the garden — into a cinema screen, which makes it the single biggest upgrade a film lover can get. It is the gift for the family that hosts movie nights and the teenager whose bedroom becomes the house cinema. On Cyber Monday, a big-ticket item like this makes sense: it is one of the most expensive picks here, and it is the one the whole household shares for years. Set it up an hour before showtime and the evening plans itself. More gift ideas for couples.

2. Beginner's Telescope ($113.49)

Winter nights are long and dark, which is exactly why a telescope makes such a good Christmas gift. The Beginner's Telescope ($113.49) is built for first-timers: simple to set up, easy to aim, and strong enough to show the Moon's craters and the bright planets in real detail. It is the gift for the curious child, the teenager who loves space, and the adult who has always wanted to look up properly. Give it with a simple star chart and you have given them a hobby, not just an object. More gift ideas for kids.

3. Smart Digital Photo Frame ($65.97)

Some gifts keep giving without anyone having to do anything. The Smart Digital Photo Frame ($65.97) shows a rotating display of family photos that can be updated from a phone — new pictures appear on the frame without anyone touching it. It is the gift for grandparents who want to see the grandchildren grow up, for parents whose walls are already full of frames, and for anyone who loves photographs but never prints them. Buy it early, load it with your favourite photos before wrapping it, and it is ready to delight the moment it is switched on. More gifts for parents.

4. Waterproof Bluetooth Speaker ($59.29)

A good speaker goes everywhere the music goes, and this one goes further than most. The Waterproof Bluetooth Speaker ($59.29) plays music in the kitchen, in the garden, and even beside the bath or the pool without a single worry about splashes. It is the gift for the person who cooks with a playlist on, the friend who hosts summer barbecues, and the teenager whose shower singing deserves better sound. One speaker, used by the whole household, every single day. More gift ideas for teens.

5. Temperature-Control Smart Mug ($30.09)

Everyone knows the small tragedy of a hot drink gone cold: you make it, life interrupts, and by the time you return it is lukewarm. The Temperature-Control Smart Mug ($30.09) solves that properly by holding a drink at the chosen temperature, so the last sip is as good as the first. It is a brilliant gift for the person who works from home and the early riser who nurses one long coffee. Practical, clever, and used every single day — the three qualities of a gift that never disappoints. At just over thirty dollars, it is also the easiest pick on this list to buy for someone you do not know well. More gift ideas for him.

6. Smart Health Tracker Ring ($24.99)

Not everyone wants a smartwatch, but plenty of people want the data one gives — and that is exactly the gap this little ring fills. The Smart Health Tracker Ring ($24.99) tracks sleep, steps, and daily activity from a finger, with no screen to charge every night and no bulky watch on the wrist. It is the gift for the runner who already owns a watch, the friend who is trying to sleep better, and the teenager who is curious about their own fitness numbers. It looks like jewellery, works like a health tracker, and costs less than most smartwatch straps. More gift ideas for her.

7. Astronaut LED Star Projector Night Light ($9.25)

The smallest price on this list, and one of the most charming gifts in our whole collection. The Astronaut LED Star Projector Night Light ($9.25) is a little astronaut that projects stars across a bedroom ceiling — part night light, part decoration, and pure delight for a child. It is the gift for the kid who is fascinated by space, the perfect stocking filler, and the extra present that makes a bigger gift feel like a set. It also works as a gentle bedtime light long after the novelty wears off. Under ten dollars, it is the easiest yes on this list. More Christmas gift ideas.

How to pick a Cyber Monday gift

Pick the gift that matches how the person actually spends their time. The projector and the speaker are for the household that gathers around films and music. The telescope and the astronaut projector are for the ones who look up at the sky. The photo frame is for the family that loves its memories, the smart mug is for the slow-morning person, and the health ring is for the one who is always moving. Buy during the Cyber Monday sales, and check the delivery estimate at checkout so everything lands before the 18 December shipping cutoff. Not sure which fits? Our quiz sorts it in 60 seconds.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Cyber Monday gift? A tech gift people use every day — a mini projector, a smart photo frame, or a Bluetooth speaker. The best buys are the ones already on your list, now at the cheapest price of the year.

When is Cyber Monday 2026? Cyber Monday falls on 30 November 2026, two days after Black Friday. The AliExpress sale runs to 3 December, and Daraz Blessed Friday runs 26 November to 3 December, so the deals keep going past Monday itself.

Will these gifts arrive before Christmas? Order by around 18 December for the best chance of Christmas delivery, and always check the delivery estimate at checkout before you buy.

Are these gifts only for tech lovers? No — most of them suit anyone. The photo frame, the smart mug, and the astronaut projector need no technical skill at all, which makes them safe choices for grandparents and kids alike.

Buy them while Cyber Monday prices last, and give the people on your list a gift they will actually use every day.
