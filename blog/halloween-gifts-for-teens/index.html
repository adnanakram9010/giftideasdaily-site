Halloween Gifts for Teens: 7 Picks They'll Actually Like

By Gift Ideas Daily Editors · Oct 30, 2026 · 5 min read

Buying a Halloween gift for a teenager is a different kind of challenge. Too childish and it gets ignored, too grown-up and it feels like a chore, and anything that is pure novelty usually ends up in a drawer by November. The sweet spot is a gift that fits the Halloween mood — the party, the costumes, the late night with friends — but still earns its place afterwards. With Halloween falling tomorrow, Saturday 31 October, there is still time to pick something good, as long as you choose a gift that does a real job. Here are seven picks from our collection that teenagers actually like, all ready to make the weekend better. Browse our full Halloween gift collection for even more spooky-season ideas.

1. Retro Handheld Game Console ($47.19)

Every teenager knows the newest games, but far fewer own a console that plays the classics — and that is exactly why this one lands so well. The Retro Handheld Game Console comes loaded with old-school titles, fits in a pocket, and needs no television, no downloads and no Wi-Fi. It is perfect for the quiet stretches of a Halloween night: between films, while waiting for friends to arrive, or on the way home after the party. It also travels well, so it keeps working on school trips, long car rides and lazy Sundays long after the costumes are packed away. For the teen who already seems to own every new release, a pocketful of gaming history is a genuinely fresh surprise.

2. Waterproof Bluetooth Speaker ($65.89)

No Halloween playlist, no party — and a teenager's phone speaker was never going to carry a whole room. The Waterproof Bluetooth Speaker is loud enough for dancing, tough enough to survive a garden, a kitchen spill or a rain shower, and easy enough that anyone can connect and take over the music. Let the birthday teen build the playlist before guests arrive: a mix of classic Halloween songs, film themes and a few dance tracks, and the atmosphere takes care of itself. The best part is that this is not a one-night gift. It becomes the speaker for every gathering that follows, from study sessions to summer barbecues, which makes it one of the most-used gifts on this list.

3. Galaxy Star Projector Light ($25.69)

If a teenager's room is their world, this is the gift that changes how it feels. The Galaxy Star Projector Light fills a ceiling with drifting stars and soft nebula clouds, turning an ordinary bedroom into a place that feels calm, cinematic and a little bit magical. On Halloween night it sets the mood for a film marathon or a small gathering far better than harsh overhead lights. After Halloween it simply stays: a wind-down light for homework breaks, a background glow for gaming, and a favourite for anyone who finds it hard to switch off at night. It is the rare spooky-season gift that a teenager will still switch on every single evening in December.

4. Smart LED Strip Lights ($45.91)

Ask any teenager what would improve their room and coloured lighting is usually near the top of the list. The Smart LED Strip Lights stick along a desk, a bed frame or a shelf and can be set to orange and purple for Halloween, then changed to any colour afterwards. That flexibility is the whole point: one gift covers the party look, the film-night look and the everyday look, all controlled from a phone. They photograph beautifully too, which matters when the Halloween costume photos go online. For a teen who loves redecorating their space without spending much, this is a gift that keeps reinventing itself every time they change the colour.

5. Build-Your-Own Robot Kit ($25.18)

Not every teenager wants a Halloween gift that glows or plays music. Some want something to actually do, and this kit is built for them. The Build-Your-Own Robot Kit turns an afternoon into a proper project: following the steps, solving the fiddly bits, and ending up with a robot they made themselves. It is a great gift for the weekend itself, giving a hands-on teen something absorbing to do before the evening's plans, and it becomes a talking point when friends come round. It also suits the teen who is curious about how things work far better than another novelty item, because the finished robot is proof of what they built, not just something they unwrapped.

6. DIY Gel Nail Starter Kit with UV LED Lamp ($57.39)

Halloween is one of the best excuses of the year for bold nails — deep black, blood red, ghostly white, or a full set of tiny pumpkins — and this kit lets a teenager do it properly at home. The DIY Gel Nail Starter Kit with UV LED Lamp includes everything needed for a salon-style gel manicure, with the lamp to set each coat, so the result lasts far longer than ordinary polish. It is a gift that works brilliantly as a pre-party activity with friends, each person trying a different design, and it keeps paying off afterwards: no more paying for a salon visit every time a new colour is wanted. For a teen who loves self-expression through style, this is both a Halloween treat and a genuinely useful kit.

7. Halloween Ghost Glowing Night Light ($13.25)

Sometimes the best gift is the small one that makes someone smile every night. The Halloween Ghost Glowing Night Light is a friendly little ghost that glows softly on a desk or bedside table — spooky enough for Halloween, gentle enough to leave on all year. It is the ideal add-on gift, the thing you buy alongside a bigger present, or the perfect choice when you want to mark the occasion without spending much. It also solves a real problem: a soft light for a teenager who reads late, games late, or simply prefers a room that is never completely dark. Small, sweet and useful, it is the kind of gift that quietly becomes a favourite.

Teenagers are hard to buy for because they can tell the difference between a gift chosen for them and a gift chosen for the occasion. Every pick above passes the same test: it fits Halloween weekend, and it still matters on an ordinary Tuesday in November. If you are still torn between two of them, take our gift quiz to narrow it down, or browse more gifts for teens to compare the full range before tomorrow night.
