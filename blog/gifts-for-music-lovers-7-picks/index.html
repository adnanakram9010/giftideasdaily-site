Gifts for Music Lovers: 7 Picks They'll Love

By Gift Ideas Daily Editors · Nov 13, 2026 · 6 min read

The best gift for a music lover is something that makes their listening better — a speaker that fills the room, earbuds that silence the world, or a keepsake of the song that means the most to them. That is the short answer. Music lovers are easy to shop for once you stop guessing at albums and start buying for how they actually listen: on the move, in the shower, at a desk, or with friends singing along. This list has seven gifts from our own GiftIdeasDaily collection, from under seven dollars to under eighty, chosen for every kind of listener — and with the 11.11 sale still running until 18 November, now is a good time to buy before Black Friday prices arrive. Browse our full Christmas gift guide alongside this list.

1. Waterproof Bluetooth Speaker ($59.19)

Every music lover has a speaker — but the one they actually use everywhere is the one that survives real life. The Waterproof Bluetooth Speaker ($59.19) goes from the kitchen to the garden to the shower without a second thought, which is exactly why it gets used daily while the fancy living-room speaker gathers dust. At under sixty dollars it is the strongest all-rounder on this list: loud enough for a room, tough enough for outdoors, and the kind of present that turns an ordinary evening into a small party. 2. Noise-Cancelling Earbuds ($79.99)

For the listener who treats music as a private world, nothing beats a good pair of earbuds that shuts the noise out. The Noise-Cancelling Earbuds ($79.99) are the premium pick on this list — the gift for the commuter, the student who studies with a soundtrack, or the partner who wants to hear the album, not the traffic. If you are buying one big gift this Christmas, this is the one that feels like a real upgrade rather than just another gadget. More gift ideas for teens.

3. Custom Song Plaque ($10.95)

Some gifts are about sound. This one is about the song itself. The Custom Song Plaque ($10.95) turns their favourite song — the first-dance song, the road-trip anthem, the one they played a hundred times — into wall art with the album art and a scannable code. It is personal without being expensive, which makes it the best stocking stuffer on this list and a lovely choice for a partner, a best friend, or anyone with a song that tells your story together. At under eleven dollars, pair it with a handwritten note about why that song matters and you have a gift they will keep on the wall for years. More gift ideas for her.

4. Mini Bluetooth Speaker (FM + MP3) ($6.99)

Not every gift needs to be big to be used every day. The Mini Bluetooth Speaker (FM + MP3) ($6.99) is the pocket-sized pick: small enough for a desk, a bag, or a bedside table, with radio and MP3 playback built in. It is perfect for the teenager who wants their own speaker, the colleague who needs desk music, or as the second gift tucked inside a bigger present. Under seven dollars it is also the easiest gift on this list to buy in multiples — one for each friend, one for the stocking, one for the Secret Santa draw. More gifts for teens.

5. Haomuren Mini Karaoke Machine for Kids (All-in-One) ($21.56)

Music lovers are made young, and this is the gift that starts it. The Haomuren Mini Karaoke Machine for Kids (All-in-One) ($21.56) is a complete karaoke setup sized for small hands — microphone, speaker, and lights in one box, ready for the first song within minutes of unwrapping. It is the gift for the child who sings into a hairbrush, the family that turns every gathering into a performance, and the parent who wants a present that gets the whole room laughing together. At under twenty-two dollars it is also a Christmas-morning winner: it works straight away, needs no setup, and the whole family ends up using it. More gift ideas for kids.

6. Galaxy Star Projector Light ($25.59)

Music sounds better in the right light — ask anyone who has ever dimmed the lamps for their favourite album. The Galaxy Star Projector Light ($25.59) fills the ceiling with drifting stars and nebula clouds, turning an ordinary listening session into a small planetarium show. It is the gift for the listener who plays vinyl in the dark, the teenager whose bedroom is their concert hall, and the couple who want their evenings to feel like an occasion. 7. Smart LED Strip Lights ($45.91)

Finish with the gift that turns a room into a venue. The Smart LED Strip Lights ($45.91) run behind the TV, along the desk, or around the bed frame, and change colour with a tap — deep red for a moody album, warm amber for a slow evening, bright colour for a party playlist. Music lovers adore them because the room finally matches the mood of what is playing. More gifts for him.

How to choose the right music gift

Three questions cover almost every music lover. First, where do they listen? The commuter needs the noise-cancelling earbuds, the shower singer needs the waterproof speaker, and the bedroom listener wants the star projector or the LED strips. Second, do they listen alone or together? Solo listeners love earbuds and plaques; the social ones want the karaoke machine or the big speaker. Third, is it about the music or the memory? If it is the memory, the custom song plaque wins every time — no speaker can beat a song that means something. Not sure which fits? Our quiz sorts it in 60 seconds.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best gift for a music lover under $60? The Waterproof Bluetooth Speaker ($59.19) is the best all-rounder — it works indoors and outdoors, gets used daily, and suits almost any listener. For a smaller budget, the Custom Song Plaque ($10.95) is the most personal pick under $11.

Are earbuds or a speaker a better gift? It depends on how they listen. Earbuds suit commuters and solo listeners who want their own world; a speaker suits hosts, families, and anyone who shares their music. When in doubt, the speaker is the safer gift — it gets used by the whole household.

What music gift works for a child? The Haomuren Mini Karaoke Machine for Kids ($21.56) — it is made for small hands, works straight out of the box, and turns a gift into a family activity instead of a solo toy.

Should I buy now or wait for Black Friday? Buy the personalised and small gifts now — the 11.11 sale runs until 18 November, and Black Friday (27 November) brings crowds and shipping queues. The speaker and earbuds are safe to grab during the sale; the song plaque needs making time, so order it early.

Music is personal, so pick the gift that fits how they actually listen. Match their habit, and it will be used every single day.
