Gifts for Gamers: 7 Picks for GTA VI Launch Day

By Gift Ideas Daily Editors · Nov 19, 2026 · 6 min read

Grand Theft Auto VI is out today — Thursday 19 November — on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and it is the biggest game launch of the year. The short answer: the best gift for a GTA VI player is not the game itself, it is something that makes playing it better — a bigger screen, better sound, or lighting that turns an ordinary room into a proper game room. Here are seven gifts from our collection that do exactly that, from $7.29 to $110.39. Browse our full Christmas gift guide alongside this list.

1. Portable Mini Projector ($110.39)

GTA VI is a game built to be seen big — a huge open world, bright neon streets, and detail that a small television simply cannot show off. The Portable Mini Projector ($110.39) turns any blank wall into a cinema-sized screen, so the game fills the whole room instead of sitting in a corner of it. It is the gift for the player who has waited years for this launch and wants launch night to feel like an event, not just another evening on the sofa. After launch week it keeps earning its place too — film nights, sports, and every big game that follows. More gift ideas for teens.

2. Waterproof Bluetooth Speaker ($59.19)

A game's soundtrack is half the experience, and a phone speaker was never going to carry it. The Waterproof Bluetooth Speaker ($59.19) is loud enough for a whole room, tough enough to survive a garden, a kitchen spill, or a knocked-over drink, and easy enough that anyone can connect and take over the music. It is the gift for the player who builds a playlist for every session — the driving music, the heist music, the songs that will be stuck in everyone's head by the weekend. More gift ideas for him.

3. Smart LED Strip Lights ($45.91)

Ask any gamer what would improve their setup and coloured lighting is usually near the top of the list. The Smart LED Strip Lights ($45.91) stick along a desk, a television stand, or a shelf, and can be set to match the game's mood — neon pink and blue for a night drive, deep red for a tense mission, calm warm white for winding down afterwards. That flexibility is the whole point: one gift covers the launch-night look and the everyday look, all controlled from a phone. For a player who loves their gaming corner, this is the gift that changes how the whole room feels. More gifts for the home.

4. Smart Health Tracker Ring ($25.59)

Here is the gift nobody expects and everybody ends up using. Launch week means late nights, and the Smart Health Tracker Ring ($25.59) quietly tracks sleep and activity on their finger, so the player can see exactly what a 2 a.m. gaming session costs the next morning. It scratches the fitness-tracker itch without the smartwatch price tag, and it looks like tech, not a health lecture. It is the gift for the gamer who is into sports or sleep tracking, and for the parent or partner who would love a gentle nudge towards balance. More gift ideas for him.

5. 3D Print Rechargeable Moon Lamp ($10.98)

Every good gaming setup has one piece of lighting that is purely for atmosphere, and this is it. The 3D Print Rechargeable Moon Lamp ($10.98) is a small glowing moon for the desk or bedside table — soft, warm, and exactly the right brightness for a room where the screen is doing the talking. It is the gift for the player whose setup already has the big screen and the good sound, and just needs that one finishing touch that makes the room feel like theirs. More gift ideas for teens.

6. Sunset Projector Lamp ($10.33)

If the moon lamp is for calm evenings, this one is for drama. The Sunset Projector Lamp ($10.33) throws a warm sunset glow across a wall, turning a plain bedroom into a scene that looks straight out of a game — golden light, long shadows, and an atmosphere that makes even a loading screen feel cinematic. It is the gift for the player who loves the look of their space as much as the games in it. Small, cheap, and used every single evening — the kind of gift that quietly becomes a favourite. More gift ideas for her.

7. Funny Magnetic Desk Toys ($7.29)

Every gamer needs something for their hands between sessions. The Funny Magnetic Desk Toys ($7.29) are a fidget-worthy desk companion — small magnetic pieces to build, balance, and play with while waiting for a download, a lobby, or a friend to come online. They are the stocking-filler price with a real job: keeping restless hands busy during the long waits that every big launch brings, including GTA VI's famously large download. More Christmas gift ideas.

How to build the perfect game room for launch night

Put it together and the formula is simple: the picture (projector), the sound (speaker), the lighting (LED strips, moon lamp, sunset lamp), and one small thing for the desk. Start with the room, not the game. Dim the overhead lights, set the strips to a colour that matches the mood, and let the lamps do the rest — the game will look better on a big screen with the right glow around it. Then think about the person, not the launch. A casual player will love the lamps and the desk toys. A serious player will notice the projector and the speaker first. Not sure which fits? Frequently asked questions

What is the best gift for a GTA VI player? Something that improves how they play: a bigger screen, better sound, or setup lighting. The Portable Mini Projector ($110.39) is the biggest upgrade on this list, while the Smart LED Strip Lights ($45.91) change how the whole room feels for less than fifty dollars.

When does GTA VI come out? GTA VI launches today, Thursday 19 November 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. There is no PC version announced yet, so check which console the person you are buying for actually owns before you gift anything console-specific.

What is a good cheap gift for a gamer? The Funny Magnetic Desk Toys ($7.29) and the Sunset Projector Lamp ($10.33) on this list both cost under eleven dollars and get used every day — the desk toys during downloads and loading screens, the lamp every evening the setup is on.

Are these gifts only for GTA VI players? No. Every pick on this list works for any gamer, and for film nights, music, and everyday use too. The projector, speaker, and lighting are room upgrades first and gaming gifts second, which is why they still get used long after launch week.

Launch day comes once, but a good setup lasts for years. Pick the gift that matches how they actually play — big screen, big sound, or better light — and let the game do the rest.
