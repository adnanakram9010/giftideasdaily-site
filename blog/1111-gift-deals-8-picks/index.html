11.11 Gift Deals: 8 Picks to Grab Before the Sale Ends

By Gift Ideas Daily Editors · Nov 9, 2026 · 6 min read

The 11.11 sale ends on 18 November, and that makes this week the cheapest moment of the year to buy Christmas gifts. That is the short answer, and it is the reason for this list: eight gift picks from our own GiftIdeasDaily collection, from $2.31 to $59.09, worth buying while the sale is still running. Every gift below is in our collection at the price listed today, and every one of them also works as a Christmas present. Browse our full Christmas gift guide alongside this list.

1. Personalized Birthstone Necklace ($4.44)

Personalised jewellery is one of the biggest sellers of the 11.11 sale — personalised necklaces alone have passed 10,000 orders on the big sale platforms this week — and this one costs less than a coffee. The Personalized Birthstone Necklace ($4.44) is made to order with the recipient's birthstone, so a gift that costs almost nothing still feels chosen just for them. It is the ideal stocking filler or secret-Santa pick. At this price, one for every woman on your list costs less than a lunch out. More gift ideas for her.

2. Galaxy Star Projector Light ($25.59)

Few gifts change a room as fast as this one. The Galaxy Star Projector Light ($25.59) fills a ceiling with drifting stars and nebula clouds, turning an ordinary bedroom into a planetarium in seconds. It suits the teenager who lives in their room, the couple who love slow evenings at home, and the child who wants the stars above their bed. It works every night of the year — a sale purchase that earns its place on a shelf for years. More gift ideas for teens.

3. Temperature-Control Smart Mug ($29.99)

Everyone knows the small tragedy of a hot drink gone cold: you make it, life interrupts, and by the time you return it is lukewarm. The Temperature-Control Smart Mug ($29.99) holds a drink at the chosen temperature, so the last sip is as good as the first. It is a brilliant gift for the person who works from home or the early riser with one long coffee. Practical, clever, and used every single day — the three qualities of a gift that never disappoints. 4. Waterproof Bluetooth Speaker ($59.09)

This is the biggest-ticket pick on the list, and the one where the sale timing matters most. The Waterproof Bluetooth Speaker ($59.09) is for the music lover who takes their playlist everywhere — the kitchen, the garden, the beach, the shower. Waterproof means it survives where other speakers do not, and portable means it gets used, not shelved. It is a proper main present — and 11.11 is the smart time to buy it. More gift ideas for him.

5. Personalized LED Neon Sign ($22.85)

A neon sign turns a plain wall into a statement, and a personalised one turns it into their wall. The Personalized LED Neon Sign ($22.85) can carry a name, a favourite word, or a short message in glowing light — the kind of gift that photographs beautifully and becomes the backdrop of every video call and party photo. It suits a new home, a teenager's bedroom, or a couple's first flat. Personalised gifts need making time, so ordering during the sale is exactly the right move: no rush fees, no production queues, no panic. More housewarming gift ideas.

6. Hand-Poured Soy Candle Set ($7.05)

Winter evenings run on atmosphere, and nothing creates it faster than candlelight. The Hand-Poured Soy Candle Set ($7.05) brings a soft glow and a gentle scent to long dark evenings — lit during dinner, beside a bath, or while the house winds down. Candles are one of the safest gifts to give: they suit almost anyone, they never need the right size, and they get used up rather than adding clutter. At seven dollars, this is the filler that makes any gift box feel finished. More gift ideas for her.

7. Custom 1000-Piece Photo Puzzle ($9.55)

Some gifts are opened and forgotten; this one takes a whole evening to enjoy. The Custom 1000-Piece Photo Puzzle ($9.55) turns a favourite family photo into a thousand-piece challenge — a slow, sociable activity for a winter night, and a framed keepsake once it is finished. It is a wonderful gift for grandparents, for puzzle lovers, and for any family that likes doing things together. At under ten dollars it is one of the best-value picks here: an evening of entertainment and a piece of wall art in a single box. More gifts for parents.

8. Engraved Leather Keychain ($2.31)

Finally, the budget hero of the whole sale. The Engraved Leather Keychain ($2.31) carries initials or a short message on real leather, and it gets touched every single day — on keys, on a backpack, on a bag. Personalised, durable, and cheaper than a greeting card, it is the gift for the person you almost forgot or the small thank-you that still needs to feel real. Buy five at once and five people are crossed off your list for the price of one takeaway coffee. More small birthday gift ideas.

How to shop the 11.11 sale without wasting money

A sale only saves money if you buy things you would have bought anyway, so use the week properly. First, make your Christmas list now and buy only what is on it. Second, buy personalised gifts first — they need production time, and the sale window is the last comfortable moment to order them. Third, check the delivery date before you pay: a bargain that arrives after Christmas is not a bargain. Remember the real deadline: the 11.11 sale ends on 18 November, but the best stock goes first. Frequently asked questions

When does the 11.11 sale end? The main 11.11 sale runs until 18 November. After that, prices return to normal and personalised gifts take longer to make, so the sale week is the best time to buy Christmas gifts.

What is the best 11.11 gift under $10? The Personalized Birthstone Necklace ($4.44), the Hand-Poured Soy Candle Set ($7.05), the Custom 1000-Piece Photo Puzzle ($9.55), and the Engraved Leather Keychain ($2.31) are all under $10 — and the necklace and the keychain are personalised, so they feel far more expensive than they are.

Are these gifts also good for Christmas? Yes. The projector, the speaker, and the neon sign are main presents; the necklace, candle set, puzzle, and keychain are stocking fillers; the smart mug suits anyone who drinks coffee or tea. Buying them now simply means paying the sale price instead of the December price.

Which gift should I buy first during the sale? The personalised ones: the birthstone necklace, the neon sign, the photo puzzle, and the keychain. They are made to order, so their production queues grow as Christmas gets closer — order during 11.11 and they arrive with weeks to spare.

The 11.11 sale is the year's best excuse to finish Christmas shopping early. Pick the gifts that match the people on your list, order the personalised ones first, and enjoy December.
