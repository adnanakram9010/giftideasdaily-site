Secret Santa Gifts Under $20: 8 Picks They'll Love

By Gift Ideas Daily Editors · Nov 10, 2026 · 6 min read

Secret Santa works best with a simple rule: spend under $20, pick something personal, and never buy a generic gift card. That is the short answer, and it is the whole idea behind this list: eight Secret Santa gifts from our own GiftIdeasDaily collection, every one of them under $20, that feel chosen rather than grabbed at the last minute. Browse our full Christmas gift guide alongside this list.

1. Funny 'World's Best' Mug Duo ($7.75)

A Secret Santa gift gets opened in front of everyone, so it needs to land in the room — and a funny mug does exactly that. The Funny 'World's Best' Mug Duo ($7.75) is a pair of mugs with a joke that works for a colleague, a friend, or a family member, and two mugs mean the recipient can keep one and share one. It is cheap, cheerful, and used every single morning, which is more than most Secret Santa gifts manage. More gift ideas for him.

2. Funny Cat Socks 5-Pack ($4.45)

Socks are only a bad gift when they are boring, and these are not boring. The Funny Cat Socks 5-Pack ($4.45) gives the recipient five pairs with genuinely funny cat designs — a gift that gets worn constantly and commented on every time a new pair appears. At under five dollars it is the cheapest pick on this list, which leaves room in the budget to pair it with a small chocolate or a handwritten card. It suits the cat lover and anyone who appreciates a gift that does not take itself too seriously. More gift ideas for her.

3. Custom Song Plaque ($10.95)

This is the pick for when you actually know the person. The Custom Song Plaque ($10.95) turns their favourite song — the one they have played hundreds of times — into wall art with the album art and a scannable code. Order it early, because a custom plaque needs making time, and a November order arrives with weeks to spare while a December order does not. For a friend or a close colleague, this is the gift that gets photographed and kept. More gift ideas for teens.

4. Funny Magnetic Desk Toys ($6.79)

Some Secret Santa gifts are opened, laughed at, and forgotten by lunch. This one stays on the desk. Funny Magnetic Desk Toys ($6.79) are a fidget-worthy companion for anyone who works at a computer — small magnets to build, balance, and play with during long calls and slow afternoons. It is the ideal office Secret Santa gift because it is made for the desk it will live on. More gift ideas for him.

5. Engraved Brass Compass ($6.65)

For the person who has everything, give them something with a message on it. The Engraved Brass Compass ($6.65) is a small, solid keepsake that can carry initials or a short message — a gift that feels far more expensive than its price because it was made for one person. It suits the traveller, the graduate, the friend starting something new, and the colleague who is hard to buy for. More gift ideas for her.

6. Custom Coordinates Bracelet ($2.89)

Here is the budget hero of this list. The Custom Coordinates Bracelet ($2.89) carries the coordinates of a place that matters — where two people met, a hometown, a favourite holiday spot — on a simple bracelet that gets worn every day. It costs less than a coffee, yet it is unmistakably personal, which is the whole secret of good Secret Santa shopping: specificity beats price every time. Buy it for the friend whose story you know, and add a note explaining the coordinates. More gift ideas for her.

7. Heart-Shaped Scented Tea Candles, Set of 9 ($4.85)

Candles are the safest Secret Santa gift in existence: they suit almost anyone, they never need the right size, and they get used up instead of adding clutter. The Heart-Shaped Scented Tea Candles (Set of 9) ($4.85) add a small twist — nine heart-shaped candles in a set that looks like a proper present rather than a single candle in a bag. They are perfect for the colleague you barely know, the neighbour in the draw, or as the second half of a two-part gift alongside the mug duo or the socks. More Christmas gift ideas.

8. Monogrammed Whiskey Glass ($8.19)

End the list with the grown-up pick. The Monogrammed Whiskey Glass ($8.19) carries a single initial on a proper glass — a small luxury the recipient would probably never buy for themselves, which is exactly what makes it a good gift. It suits the colleague who appreciates a good drink, the friend with a home bar, and the Secret Santa draw where the budget is $10 and you want the gift to look like $20. More gift ideas for him.

How to pick a Secret Santa gift that actually lands

Three rules cover almost every draw. First, buy for the person, not the budget: a $2.89 bracelet with their coordinates beats a $19.99 generic gadget every time. Second, think about where the gift will be opened — in front of a group — so pick something that reads well in public: funny, personal, or beautiful, never awkward. Third, buy early: the personalised picks on this list — the song plaque, the compass, the bracelet — need making time, and a November order arrives stress-free while a December order arrives with prayers. Frequently asked questions

What is a good Secret Santa budget? Most office and family draws set $10 to $20, and every gift on this list is under $20 — the cheapest is $2.89 and the most expensive is $10.95. A smaller budget is not a problem if the gift is specific to the person.

What is the best Secret Santa gift for a colleague I barely know? The Funny 'World's Best' Mug Duo ($7.75), the Heart-Shaped Scented Tea Candles ($4.85), or the Funny Magnetic Desk Toys ($6.79). All three are safe, funny, and useful, and none of them needs you to know anything personal about the recipient.

What is the best personalised Secret Santa gift under $20? The Custom Song Plaque ($10.95) for a music lover, the Engraved Brass Compass ($6.65) for someone starting something new, and the Custom Coordinates Bracelet ($2.89) for a friend whose story you know. Order personalised gifts in November — they need making time before Christmas.

Can these gifts double as stocking fillers? Yes. The cat socks, the tea candles, the bracelet, and the compass are all small enough for a stocking, and the mug duo and the whiskey glass work as the main small present under the tree. Buying them now, during the November sales, means paying less than in December.

Secret Santa is not a test of how much you spend. It is a test of whether you paid attention — and a $2.89 bracelet with the right coordinates proves you did. Pick the gift that matches the person, order the personalised ones first, and enjoy the moment it gets opened.
