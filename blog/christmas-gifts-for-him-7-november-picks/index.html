Christmas Gifts for Him: 7 Picks to Buy in November

By Gift Ideas Daily Editors · Nov 5, 2026 · 6 min read

Buy his Christmas gift in November, not December. That is the short answer, and it is the reason for this list: seven Christmas gifts for him, from $8.05 to $79.99, that are easy to order now and easy to wrap. November ordering matters most for the personalised picks, which need time to be made, and for anything sent by post, with the Christmas delivery rush building through December. Every gift below is in our own GiftIdeasDaily collection, at the price listed today. Browse our full Christmas gift guide alongside this list.

1. Noise-Cancelling Earbuds ($79.99)

If one gift on this list earns the title of main present, it is this one. The Noise-Cancelling Earbuds ($79.99) shut out the noise of the commute, the flight, or the house at midnight, and put him right inside the music, the podcast, or the film. They are the gift for the man who travels, the one who works in a noisy office, or the one who watches films late when everyone else is asleep. Unlike a novelty gadget, earbuds get used every single day, which makes them the most-used gift on this list. At $79.99 they are the biggest spend here, and the one most likely to be mentioned again in January. More gift ideas for him.

2. Waterproof Bluetooth Speaker ($59.19)

Some men are hard to buy for because they already own everything they need. A speaker is the exception, because the one they own is usually the phone in their pocket. The Waterproof Bluetooth Speaker ($59.19) is loud enough for the kitchen, the garden, or the garage, tough enough to survive a spill or a rain shower, and easy enough that anyone can connect and take over the music. It suits the man who hosts, the one who cooks with music on, and the one whose weekends move from room to room. It also keeps working long after Christmas, at every gathering that follows. More gift ideas for teens.

3. Custom Bobblehead Figure ($58.99)

For the man who is genuinely impossible to buy for, a gift made to look like him is almost unfairly effective. The Custom Bobblehead Figure ($58.99) is made to resemble the recipient, so it becomes a conversation piece the moment it is unwrapped and a keepsake on the desk long afterwards. It works as a birthday surprise all year round too, but as a Christmas gift it has one extra advantage: nobody else will give him the same thing. Order it first on this list, because a custom figure has to be made before it can be shipped, and November is the sensible time to buy. More birthday gift ideas.

4. Retro Handheld Game Console ($47.09)

Every man who grew up with video games remembers the classics, and far fewer own a console that still plays them. The Retro Handheld Game Console ($47.09) comes loaded with old-school titles, fits in a pocket, and needs no television, no downloads, and no Wi-Fi. It is perfect for quiet winter evenings, for long journeys, and for the man who already seems to own every new release. A pocketful of gaming history is a genuinely fresh surprise. More gift ideas for him.

5. Poker Chip Set with Carrying Case ($39.19)

Some gifts are for one person; this one is for his whole circle. The Poker Chip Set with Carrying Case ($39.19) turns an ordinary evening into a proper game night, with proper chips, a proper case, and no excuses for using coins or bottle caps. It suits the man who hosts friends, the one who loves a competitive evening, and the family that gathers round a table at Christmas. At $39.19 it is a strong mid-price pick for a brother, a partner, or a friend. More gifts for couples.

6. Temperature-Control Smart Mug ($29.99)

Everyone knows the small sadness of a cup of coffee gone cold mid-task. The Temperature-Control Smart Mug ($29.99) keeps his drink at the right temperature, so the coffee he makes is still hot when he finally gets back to it. It is a practical gift with a touch of luxury, ideal for the man who works from home, the early riser who nurses one long coffee, or the tea drinker who takes an hour to finish a pot. Unlike a novelty mug, this one earns its place on the desk every day. More gifts for the home.

7. Engraved Leather Wallet ($8.05)

Some men are famously hard to buy for, and a wallet is one of the few gifts they actually use every single day. The Engraved Leather Wallet ($8.05) is a full-grain leather bifold hand-stamped with his initials, so an everyday essential becomes unmistakably his. It suits birthdays, graduations, and Father's Day as well as Christmas. At $8.05 it is also the easiest way to make a gift feel complete: add it to the smart mug or the poker set and the whole present feels planned, for less than a takeaway lunch. How to choose the right Christmas gift for him

Start with how he spends his winter evenings. The traveller and the commuter need the earbuds. The host who gathers everyone round needs the speaker or the poker set. The gamer who talks about the classics will love the retro console, and the man who is impossible to buy for is the one for the custom bobblehead. The practical man who buys quality once and keeps it for years is the one for the wallet or the smart mug. Match the gift to his habits and order it in November. Not sure which fits? Frequently asked questions

When should I order Christmas gifts for him? In November, if you can. Personalised gifts like the bobblehead and the wallet are made to order and need extra time, and posted gifts face the busiest delivery weeks of the year in December. Ordering now means it arrives with time to wrap it properly.

What is a good Christmas gift for him under $30? The Engraved Leather Wallet ($8.05) and the Temperature-Control Smart Mug ($29.99) both sit under $30, and each one feels like a real present rather than a filler. The wallet is a gift he will use every single day.

What should I buy if I do not know his taste well? Choose something useful and universal: the smart mug, the speaker, or the earbuds. They suit most tastes, they get used daily, and none of them depends on knowing his size or his style.

Which gift on this list is best as a main present? The Noise-Cancelling Earbuds ($79.99). They are the most generous pick here, they get used every day through winter and beyond, and they suit anyone who travels, commutes, or simply loves music. Pair them with the smart mug for a complete daily-use gift set.

A good Christmas gift for him is not about spending the most — it is about showing you noticed what he actually enjoys. Pick the gift that matches his winter, order it while November still gives you time, and let Christmas morning do the rest.
