Christmas Gifts for Kids: 7 Picks for Every Age

By Gift Ideas Daily Editors · Nov 7, 2026 · 6 min read

Buy your children's Christmas gifts in November, not December. That is the short answer, and it is the reason for this list: seven Christmas gifts for kids, from $9.15 to $41.49, that are easy to order now and easy to wrap. The best gifts for children are not the biggest or the flashiest — they are the ones that get played with in January, not forgotten by Boxing Day. Every gift below is in our own GiftIdeasDaily collection, at the price listed today. Browse our full Christmas gift guide alongside this list.

1. Shockproof Kids' Camera ($41.49)

If one gift on this list earns the title of main present, it is this one. The Shockproof Kids' Camera ($41.49) is built for small hands and hard floors: drop-proof, simple to use, and made for a child who wants to photograph everything — the tree, the dog, their siblings pulling crackers. It comes with games built in, so it is not just a camera but a toy in its own right. It is the gift for the child who is always borrowing your phone to take pictures, and it gives them something that is theirs. More gift ideas for kids.

2. Build-Your-Own Robot Kit ($25.18)

Some children want to open a present; others want to build one. The Build-Your-Own Robot Kit ($25.18) turns a quiet afternoon into a proper project: following the steps, solving the fiddly bits, and ending up with a robot that actually moves. It is a wonderful gift for the child who likes making things and the one who takes toys apart to see how they work. The finished robot is proof of what they built, not just something they unwrapped — and that makes it a gift they will talk about long after Christmas. More gift ideas for kids.

3. Kids Drawing Projector Table ($26.99)

For the child who is always drawing, this is the gift that turns a hobby into a proper activity. The Kids Drawing Projector Table ($26.99) projects pictures for a child to trace and colour, so even a beginner can produce something they are proud of — and a confident young artist gets a whole new way to practise. It suits rainy winter afternoons and the long Christmas holidays when the new toys need to keep working. It also grows with them: younger children trace simple shapes, older ones try harder pictures. More gift ideas for kids.

4. Haomuren Mini Karaoke Machine for Kids ($22.59)

Every family has a child who sings — in the car, in the bath, at full volume. The Haomuren Mini Karaoke Machine for Kids (All-in-One) ($22.59) gives that child a proper stage: a small all-in-one machine with a microphone, ready for Christmas performances in front of the whole family. It is the gift for the confident performer and the shy child who comes alive with a song. Buy it for one child and expect the adults to borrow it before the night is over. More Christmas gift ideas.

5. Dinosaur Terrarium Night Light ($9.65)

Some gifts are only for Christmas morning; this one earns its place every night of the year. The Dinosaur Terrarium Night Light ($9.65) is part night light, part desk pet: a small glowing dinosaur habitat that makes a dark bedroom feel magical rather than scary. It is perfect for the younger child who still wants a gentle light beside the bed, and for the dinosaur fan who will love it on the shelf even in daylight. At $9.65 it is also the easiest way to make a stocking feel considered: small, sweet, and used long after the decorations come down. More gift ideas for kids.

6. Personalized Kids Backpack ($10.15)

A backpack with their name on it is a gift a child uses every single day — for school, for trips, for carrying their favourite things everywhere they go. The Personalized Kids Backpack ($10.15) is printed with the child's name, so it is unmistakably theirs and far less likely to be left behind or mixed up with someone else's. It suits a child starting school or any child who loves having their own things in their own bag. This is also the pick to order first: personalised items are made to order, so November is the sensible time to buy. More gift ideas for kids.

7. Mini Magnetic Tile Toys ($9.15)

Finally, the gift that keeps a child busy for hours without a screen. The Mini Magnetic Tile Toys ($9.15) are colourful magnetic pieces that click together into houses, towers, animals, and whatever else a child's imagination comes up with — then come apart and start again. They suit younger children learning shapes and colours, and older ones building bigger, bolder structures. They are quiet and open-ended — the kind of toy parents are happy to see under the tree. At $9.15 they are also a perfect stocking filler. More gift ideas for kids.

How to choose the right Christmas gift for a child

Start with how they actually spend their time. The photographer who borrows your phone needs the shockproof camera. The builder who takes things apart needs the robot kit. The artist who is always drawing needs the projector table. The performer who sings everywhere needs the karaoke machine. The younger child who wants a gentle light at bedtime needs the dinosaur night light, the schoolchild needs the personalised backpack, and the quiet builder needs the magnetic tiles. Match the gift to the child, not just to their age, and order it in November. Not sure which fits? Our quiz sorts it in 60 seconds.

Frequently asked questions

When should I order Christmas gifts for kids? In November, if you can. Personalised gifts like the backpack are made to order and need extra time, and popular toys sell steadily through the festive season. Ordering now means the gift arrives with time to wrap it properly.

What is a good Christmas gift for kids under $30? The Dinosaur Terrarium Night Light ($9.65), the Mini Magnetic Tile Toys ($9.15), the Personalized Kids Backpack ($10.15), the Haomuren Mini Karaoke Machine for Kids ($22.59), the Build-Your-Own Robot Kit ($25.18), and the Kids Drawing Projector Table ($26.99) all sit under $30.

What should I buy if I do not know the child well? Choose something open-ended and universal: the magnetic tile toys, the dinosaur night light, or the drawing projector table. They suit most children and get used without needing to know a child's size or favourite character, and none of them depends on a screen.

Which gift on this list is best as a main present? The Shockproof Kids' Camera ($41.49). It is the most generous pick here, it gets used every day, and it gives a child something that is properly theirs — a camera, a toy, and a way to remember Christmas in their own pictures.

A good Christmas gift for a child is not about spending the most — it is about giving them something they will still reach for in January. Pick the gift that matches how they play, order it while November still gives you time, and let Christmas morning do the rest.
