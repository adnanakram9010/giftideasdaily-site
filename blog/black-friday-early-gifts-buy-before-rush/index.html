Black Friday Early Gifts: 7 Picks to Buy Before the Rush

By Gift Ideas Daily Editors · Oct 29, 2026 · 6 min read

Black Friday falls on Friday 27 November this year, which leaves about four weeks between now and the biggest shopping day of the year. That sounds like plenty of time, but anyone who has shopped for Christmas in December knows how quickly it disappears. The most popular gifts sell out first, personalised gifts need time to be made, and the last week of November turns into a scramble. Buying a few key gifts early is not about missing the deals. It is about making sure the gifts you actually want are still available when you need them. Here are seven picks from our collection worth buying before the Black Friday rush, whether you are shopping for Christmas or simply getting ahead of the season. Browse our full Christmas gift guide alongside this list.

1. Smart Digital Photo Frame ($76.96)

A digital photo frame is one of those gifts that keeps giving long after it is unwrapped, because new photos can be sent to it all year round. The Smart Digital Photo Frame ($76.96) is a wonderful choice for parents and grandparents: load it with family photos before you give it, and it becomes a living album on their mantelpiece rather than another object on a shelf. It is also one of the gifts that tends to sell out as Christmas approaches, since so many people buy it for exactly the same reason. Order it early, set it up in advance, and you have a present that looks far more thoughtful than the effort it took. More gifts for parents.

2. Portable Mini Projector ($124.39)

If your family spends winter evenings watching films together, a projector changes the whole experience. The Portable Mini Projector ($124.39) turns any blank wall into a cinema screen, which makes it a brilliant gift for a household rather than just one person. It is the kind of bigger purchase people put off until the sales, and then find out of stock in the exact week they wanted it. Buying it now means it is in the house ready for the first cold December weekend, not still in a delivery queue. Pair it with a note promising a film night, and the gift starts before Christmas Day itself. More gifts for couples.

3. Noise-Cancelling Earbuds ($79.99)

Earbuds are the rare gift that suits almost everyone: commuters, students, gym-goers, and anyone who works with headphones on all day. The Noise-Cancelling Earbuds ($79.99) block out the background noise of buses, offices, and busy households, which is why they are one of the most gifted tech items every holiday season. That popularity is exactly the problem in late November, when the best-known models sell through quickly. If someone on your list needs new earbuds, this is the gift to secure early rather than gamble on in the final week. More gifts for teens.

4. Retro Instant Camera ($99.95)

Some gifts are practical, and some are simply fun, and the Retro Instant Camera ($99.95) is firmly in the second camp. It prints photos on the spot, which makes it perfect for parties, holidays, and family gatherings where everyone wants a keepsake to take home. Teenagers love it, parents borrow it constantly, and it turns an ordinary evening into something worth photographing. Cameras like this are a classic Black Friday sell-out: they look great in a gift guide, everyone adds one to a list, and then the stock is gone. Buy it while it is available and keep it wrapped until the big day. More gift ideas for her.

5. Personalized Family Tree Print ($54.95)

Here is the rule that matters most for early shopping: anything personalised must be bought early, because it has to be made before it can be shipped. The Personalized Family Tree Print ($54.95) is a beautiful example, a custom piece showing the whole family that hangs on the wall for years. Ordered now, it arrives with weeks to spare. Ordered in late November, it becomes a race against production queues and delivery cut-offs. If there is one gift on this list to buy this week rather than next month, it is this one. More keepsakes for anniversaries.

6. Custom Bobblehead Figure ($59.09)

For the person who is impossible to buy for, a custom gift made to look like them is almost unfairly effective. The Custom Bobblehead Figure ($59.09) is made to resemble the recipient, which makes it a conversation piece from the moment it is unwrapped and a keepsake long afterwards. Like the family tree print, it needs production time, so it belongs firmly in the buy-early pile. It works as a birthday surprise all year round too, but as a Christmas gift it has one extra advantage: nobody else will give them the same thing. More birthday gift ideas.

7. Smart LED Strip Lights ($45.91)

Finally, a gift that upgrades a whole room in an evening. The Smart LED Strip Lights ($45.91) go behind a television, along a desk, or around a bed frame, and change the feel of the space with a tap on a phone. They are a favourite with teenagers and students, but they suit anyone who spends evenings at home in winter. They are also a sensible early buy: small, easy to wrap, easy to post, and useful from the day they are opened rather than something that waits for an occasion. Buy a set now and one more name comes off your list before November even begins. More gifts for the home.

Buy early, or wait for the day?

The honest answer is: do both, but for different gifts. Buy personalised gifts and proven sell-outs now, like the family tree print, the bobblehead, and the photo frame. Leave generic, always-in-stock items for Black Friday itself, when a discount is most likely. What you should not do is leave everything until the last week of November and hope for the best. Pick two or three names off your list today, order the gifts that need time or tend to sell out, and let the rest of November feel calm instead of frantic. Not sure which fits?
