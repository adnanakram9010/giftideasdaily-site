Black Friday Gifts Under $25: 8 Picks That Feel Like a Steal

By Gift Ideas Daily Editors · Nov 18, 2026 · 6 min read

Can you actually win Black Friday on a $25 budget? Yes — if you shop small, warm and personal. The short answer: skip the big-ticket tech everyone fights over and buy gifts people use every day in December — a warm hat-and-gloves set, a personalised ornament, a tea sampler for slow evenings. Here are eight Christmas-ready gifts from our collection, all under $25, from $4.04 to $24.52. How to shop Black Friday on a $25 budget

Start with the person, not the discount. A 50% off deal on something they do not want is money wasted, while a $4 snowflake light for an early decorator is a gift they will remember. Buy the personalised picks first — the ornament and the song plaque need making time, so order them in Black Friday week, well before the 18 December shipping cutoff. Then fill the gaps with the ready-to-ship picks: the hand warmers, hat set, tea sampler and mug duo need no making time. And set a hard rule before you browse: one gift per person, under $25 each. Frequently asked questions

Are Black Friday gifts under $25 actually good? Yes — the best ones are small, warm and personal rather than cheap tech. A $17 hat-and-gloves set gets used daily, while a discounted gadget nobody asked for ends up in a drawer.

When should I buy Christmas gifts on Black Friday? Buy personalised gifts first, in Black Friday week, because they need making time. Ready-to-ship gifts can wait until 27 November, but popular ones sell out — order before the 18 December shipping cutoff either way.

What is the best Black Friday gift under $10? The Christmas Fairy Light Snowflake LED String Light ($4.04) for the decorator, or the Funny 'World's Best' Mug Duo ($8.55) for a coffee lover — both cost less than a takeaway lunch and get used every day in December.

Should I wait for Cyber Monday instead? For these picks, no. Cyber Monday suits tech and big-ticket items; small gifts like these rarely get cheaper, and the personalised ones need the extra week. Buy them now; save Cyber Monday for anything still on your list.

Black Friday rewards the shopper with a list, not the biggest budget. Eight gifts, all under $25, all Christmas-ready — pick the one that matches the person and order the personalised ones first.
