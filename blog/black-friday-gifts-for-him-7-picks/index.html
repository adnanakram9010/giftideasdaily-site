Black Friday Gifts for Him: 7 Picks He'll Actually Use

By Gift Ideas Daily Editors · Nov 21, 2026 · 6 min read

Buying a gift for a man who says he wants nothing is the hardest part of Christmas shopping — so here is the short answer: buy him something he will actually use, and buy it during Black Friday week while it costs less. The seven picks below are all real gifts from our collection, priced from $8.19 to $38.59, and they come to $136.46 in total. AliExpress Black Friday runs 19 November to 3 December, and the main sale starts tomorrow, 22 November — so this is the right moment to order. The Whiskey Decanter Gift Set ($14.95) is a proper decanter with glasses that turns a bottle of whiskey into a small ceremony — it looks right on a shelf, on a desk, or on the drinks tray at a Christmas party. It is the gift for the man who enjoys a drink at the end of the day. Under fifteen dollars, it is the best value on this list. More gift ideas for him.

2. Monogrammed Whiskey Glass ($8.19)

Personalised gifts beat generic ones every time. The Monogrammed Whiskey Glass ($8.19) is a whiskey glass with his initials on it — his glass, not just a glass, which is exactly the point. It pairs naturally with the decanter set above: buy both and you have a complete whiskey gift for about twenty-three dollars, which looks far more generous than either gift on its own. It is also the safe choice when you are not sure what he likes, because a glass with his own initials on it is never the wrong gift. More gift ideas for him.

3. Personalized Golf Balls, 12-Pack ($10.43)

Golfers lose balls — every golfer, at every level — so a dozen personalised ones is a gift that is genuinely useful. The Personalized Golf Balls (12-Pack) ($10.43) are a set of twelve golf balls with his name or initials on them, so they stand out on the course and are easy to spot in the rough. At under a dollar a ball, this is the cheapest gift on the list for the man who spends his weekends on the course, and the most likely to be used the very next time he plays. A proper poker set makes game night feel like a real event. It is the gift for the man who hosts poker night, the friend group that meets every Friday, and the dad who deals the cards at family gatherings. More gift ideas for him.

5. Full-Grain Leather Laptop Sleeve ($24.57)

A leather sleeve is the upgrade that lasts for years. The Full-Grain Leather Laptop Sleeve ($24.57) is a slim, full-grain leather sleeve that protects a laptop from scratches and knocks while looking like something a professional carries — not a plastic case, but a proper piece of kit. 6. Portable Mini Massage Gun ($16.34)

After a long day at work, after the gym, or after a round of golf, a massage gun is the gift that gets used the same evening it is opened. The Portable Mini Massage Gun ($16.34) is a small, pocket-sized massager that eases tired shoulders, sore legs, and stiff necks — small enough to keep in a desk drawer or a gym bag, strong enough to actually help. It is the gift for the man who never buys these things for himself — a treat he would not choose, but one he will use. More gift ideas for him.

7. Engraved Watch Display Box ($23.39)

A man who owns more than one watch needs somewhere to keep them, and a display box turns a drawer full of watches into a collection. The Engraved Watch Display Box ($23.39) is a box that holds and shows off his watches, engraved to make it his — it keeps them safe, organised, and visible, instead of tangled in a drawer. It is the gift for the man who collects watches, and for the man who owns just two or three good ones and wants to look after them properly. More gift ideas for him.

How to pick a gift for him this Black Friday

The rule that ties this list together is simple: pick one thing he will use, not one thing that will sit on a shelf. The decanter set and the monogrammed glass are for the man who enjoys a drink. The golf balls are for the man who spends his weekends outside, and the poker set is for the man who hosts game night. The laptop sleeve and the massage gun are for the man who works hard and deserves something that makes his day easier. And the watch box is for the man who already has good taste and just needs somewhere to keep it. If you are buying for more than one man, mix and match: the decanter set plus the glass is a complete whiskey gift for $23.14, and the golf balls plus the poker set is a complete game-night gift for $49.02.
